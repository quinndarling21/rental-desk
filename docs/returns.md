# Return check-in

How a return is handled at the rental counter today, in Burbank, Atlanta and Albuquerque.

## At the counter

1. Pull the RA in Rental Desk. Find the agreement on the Agreements list (search by RA number or production), open it and choose Return check-in.
2. Count the items against the list of items still out on the agreement.
3. Mark each item that came back as Returned.
4. Give each item a quick visual check and set its condition: OK, Needs service or Damaged.
5. Write notes on the paper return slip and in the notes field in Rental Desk. Rental Desk requires a note for anything marked Needs service or Damaged.
6. The runner initials the paper slip. The counter staff member enters their initials in Rental Desk.
7. Complete the return. The returned lines close, and the agreement shows Returned once every item is back.

Condition is recorded with the dropdown and the notes field; the counter does not take photos at check-out or return.

## After the return

- Gear marked Needs service or Damaged goes to the service bench and shows as Service bench in Rental Desk. Gear marked OK shows as Available again. The bench usually turns gear around in 1 to 3 days.
- Each item marked Needs service or Damaged is flagged to the counter lead for the location: Luis Ibarra in Burbank, Tasha Greene in Atlanta, Ray Delgado in Albuquerque. The agreement shows it as "Flagged to" the lead.
- When the bench finds damage, the counter lead checks the return slip and opens a damage charge with the production.
- Disputed charges go in the Damage Dispute Log.
